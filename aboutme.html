Work About Play
Ginny Dong

Ginny is a UX designer with a background in human factors psychology and visual arts. She studies how people think and decide, and turn that into interfaces that are clear and easy to trust.

When I'm not designing, I'm usually chasing light with my camera, planning my next trip, or playing guitar in a band with my friends. I like going to new places and trying new media in my art — each one turns a piece of my life into something I haven't experienced before.

ginnydong2005@gmail.com

I really like trying out different media. A big part of the fun is not always knowing how something will turn out. Different materials can give you completely different results, so it sometimes feels a little like opening a blind box. I enjoy experimenting, making mistakes, and seeing what happens along the way. It keeps making art fun for me because there's always something new to try.

I really enjoy shooting film and developing it myself. I like how the whole process feels slower and more intentional. Since a roll only has around 36 shots, I become much more careful about what I choose to photograph. If I take a thousand digital photos, I might never look back at most of them, but with film, the time and effort I put into each roll makes every photo feel more meaningful and worth keeping.

I start planning the next one before I've unpacked the last. Growing up between places made moving feel like the default setting.

I play bass and guitar in a band. We mostly cover songs from bands we like, and every once in a while we try writing something of our own. I honestly don't understand music theory at all, but I still really enjoy playing with everyone. Being on stage together is probably my favorite part — it's just fun, a little chaotic, and always feels different from practicing alone.

The in-between things I keep taking pictures of — a bowl that came out crooked, a bus window, a good afternoon.
